# Federal agency fitness standards for GS-1811 candidates

Access date for every URL below: 2026-11-24. This file is the single owner of federal-agency fitness-test numbers; the older note `domain-1811-hiring.md` is not edited and its fitness figures are superseded by this one.

Evidence levels: **read** = I fetched the official page/PDF text myself. **excerpt** = official page was blocked (HTTP 403) or unreadable, figure comes from a search-result summary of that official page; re-verify live. **secondary** = non-agency source, only where no primary found. No numbers here are copied from full scoring tables; links point to the official tables.

## Summary
- Fitness tests are NOT uniform: at least 8 distinct pre-employment tests exist (FBI, DEA, ATF, USSS, ICE/HSI, State DS, USPIS, USMS), each with different events, scoring systems and pass rules. A single "federal standard" cannot be hard-coded.
- Three scoring models: fixed per-event minimums (ICE/HSI, ATF, USMS, State DS, USPIS), point systems with a total plus per-event floor (FBI, DEA, USSS), and percentile on the FLETC PEB (NCIS, FWS, many OIGs).
- Only two pass marks were confirmed by me directly from an official page (read): ICE/HSI PFT-P (page dated 02/06/2025) and USSS APAT (cumulative 20 points, no zeros; page undated). Everything else is excerpt-level.
- FBI changed its test in Nov 2025 (pull-ups replace sit-ups; total reportedly 12 -> 10 points). Official numbers could not be fetched; the figure is excerpt/secondary. Treat FBI as "link out" in v1.
- DEA 2026 guide (Version 7) is reported to drop the 300 m sprint from the hiring test and require 12 points (excerpt); older guides conflict and "applying under an earlier announcement" may use the old standard.
- ATF, USMS and State DS publish age/sex-banded minimums; ATF and USMS minimums for men 20-29 / women 20-29 are identical in the excerpts (see Contradictions: possibly same table, needs live check).
- Several agencies have NO published pre-employment fitness test (IRS-CI, TIGTA, most OIGs, EPA-CID, NOAA, AFOSI, CGIS, Army CID civilian): they require a pre-employment medical and fitness thereafter (annual PEB/own PFT). Check each USAJOBS announcement.
- FLETC CITP has no published pass/fail PT test in what I could read: it requires Physical Performance Requirements (PPR) certification on arrival and teaches the PEB; the sending agency usually sets any PT pass mark (e.g., ICE: PFT three times during CITP/HSISAT).
- Body-composition rules for entry: none found as a published numeric pre-employment cutoff at any agency. Body fat appears only in the FLETC PEB and agency post-hire annual programs. The universal rule is the OPM 1811 medical standard.
- The tool should present each agency's own scale and link/date-stamp it, not convert between agencies.

## Per-agency notes

**FBI.** Fitness is a pass/fail gate inside Phase II (also includes a written assessment). The numbers are the least certain in this file: official pages (testpp.fbijobs.gov, www.fbijobs.gov, PFT self-evaluation PDF) all returned 403 to me. Search excerpts say (1) pull-ups replaced sit-ups from Nov 2025, (2) the total required dropped from 12 to 10 points effective after 2025-11-02, (3) 1-point thresholds roughly: pull-ups F 1 / M 2-3; 300 m M ~52.4-51.1 s, F ~64.9-62.5 s; push-ups M 30-32, F 14-18; 1.5 mi M 12:24-12:15, F 13:59-13:35. Retake rule: under 6 points or a zero-point event after the first try = withdrawn, may reapply; failing in the allotted window = permanent disqualification (excerpt). Secondary reporting (Fox News, Military.com, Stew Smith) corroborates the pull-up change only. Do not hard-code FBI numbers; link to the live page.

**DEA.** The Test Preparation Guide has had at least versions 5, 6, 7 (2025-2026). Version 7 (file path dated 2026-07) is described in excerpt as: effective for the July 2026 announcement, 12-point minimum, events sit-ups/push-ups/1.5-mile run, run points doubled, at least 1 point in each, 300 m sprint not given during hiring. Scores are sex-specific, not age-specific (Feb 2023 guide, excerpt). The Feb 2023 guide reported a 36% initial PTA fail rate since 2017 (excerpt; DEA's own statement) and says the threshold could be 9 for a conditional offer vs 12 for final. Applicants "processing under announcements prior to July 2026 may have a different scoring standard" (excerpt), so the tool must ask which announcement.

**ATF.** Published table by age band and sex (three events, same order as ICE). Page marked updated 2024-11-19 in the excerpt; the page is the official table (link, do not copy full table). One ATF document says the test is repeated during week 1 of CITP (excerpt, older).

**USSS.** Four events, each 0-8, total of 20 with no zero. The per-event raw-score-to-points table was not read. I did not find the APAT date; the linked study guide appears to be a 2021 upload. Plan: link and mark "check date".

**ICE HSI.** Clearest published standard: a flat standard for everyone, no age/sex banding (page read, dated 2025-02-06). Policy 6006.1 (applicant and trainee fitness testing) appeared in search but fetch returned 404 for the URL I tried; the 2022 HSI handbook (excerpt) says selectees must pass within 90 days before reporting to the academy and trainees pass the PFT three times across CITP and HSISAT. USAJOBS announcement 882959600 (read, closed 2026-09-09) confirms: PFT before entry on duty, again in basic training, two failures = removed.

**State DSS.** Age/sex-banded fixed minimums, altitude-adjusted run times (excerpt). Older 2016 tables used percentile categories (Contradictions). Exact process stage not verified.

**USPIS.** FAST is mandatory for applicants and Basic Inspector Training students; three consecutive failures removes an applicant (excerpt). Points-based per-event (100-point scale). The existing note calls it "Applicant Physical Assessment" with a third component and "Certificate of Wellness"; the FAST excerpt lists exactly three events.

**USMS.** Published age/sex minimums; applicants must pass "immediately before" hire/academy and again to graduate. Series question: USMS career pages say Deputy US Marshal; an old USMS policy doc is reported to say GS-082 (per old note, not re-verified here). Treat USMS as "1811-adjacent, verify series."

**NCIS, FWS, OIGs.** Use the FLETC PEB at the 25th percentile of age/sex tables; the FLETC tables are reference tables ("should not be considered absolute scores," excerpt), and each agency decides how to use them.

**DCIS.** Own PRT with "fair" cutoff; DCIS also states age 21-37 with veterans' waiver (excerpt). DoD OIG USAJOBS text says a pre-employment physical readiness test is required and must be passed for duration of employment (excerpt, search summary).

## FLETC (physical training/assessment in CITP)
- CITP: 59 training days at Glynco; attendees must meet their agency's recruitment standards AND the FLETC Physical Performance Requirements (PPR) before arriving (read: https://www.fletc.gov/criminal-investigator-training-program). Applicants do not apply to FLETC.
- PPR = an established minimum physical performance standard for eligibility to a course. Students complete a PPR certification reviewed by FLETC Health Unit at start; false statements can lead to dismissal (read: https://www.fletc.gov/physical-performance-requirements).
- For CITP the FLETC PPR form (FY2023, 10/2022) assigns Group A (all), B (1-21, 23, 30), C (1-6, 8), D (1-8), E (1-16, 19) (excerpt). Example items: short-distance running, handcuffing/searching, standing/walking long periods, kneel-and-rise repeatedly, full range of motion, color vision, night vision. Form version is 2022-10; confirm current.
- Curriculum includes "Physical Efficiency Battery," "Introduction to Physical Training," and "Lifestyle Management" (read, CITP page). Physical Techniques Division phone listed on FLETC site: (912) 267-2405.
- PEB: five components - flexibility (sit-and-reach), a percent-of-body-weight push measure (bench press), 1.5-mile run, agility run (Illinois), and body fat percentage; 75% or higher in all categories excluding body composition earns a Fitness Certificate, 90% a Distinguished Fitness Certificate (excerpt, https://www.fletc.gov/peb-scores). Score tables exist by sex and 5-year age band; I did not read them. They are the source of the "25th percentile" agency rules.
- There is NO published CITP pass/fail PEB cutoff in anything I could read. Agencies set it (NCIS 25th percentile; IRS-CI says PEB in week 1; ICE requires PFT passes in training; ATF repeats its PTT in week 1). CITP PEB minimums for an arbitrary agency are therefore (unverified).

## Agencies with no published pre-employment fitness test
Searched, no pre-employment test numbers found. "Medical" = OPM 1811 medical standard applies.
- IRS-CI: see table; medical screening and certification pre-hire; annual PFT after hire.
- TIGTA: USAJOBS announcements require a pre-employment physical exam and "maintain a level of physical fitness" per agency policy (excerpt: https://www.usajobs.gov/job/845107400). No test found.
- USAID OIG and most civilian OIGs: periodic PEB after hire; "requirements vary by vacancy" (read: https://oig.usaid.gov/node/1343). VA OIG postings: medical exam only (excerpt). DoD OIG/DCIS: own PRT (table).
- AFOSI civilian: FAQ says moderate to arduous exertion, medical exams; no test (excerpt: https://www.osi.af.mil/AFOSI-Careers/Civilians/FAQs/).
- Army CID civilian 1811, CGIS, FDA-OCI, NPS Investigative Services Branch: nothing found; (unverified). Military CID (MOS 31D) is not 1811 and uses Army standards.
- EPA-CID: pre-employment medical by a medical officer; participation in fitness program after hire, up to 3 hrs/week (excerpt: https://www.epa.gov/enforcement/special-agent-eligibility-requirements).
- NOAA OLE: "Medical and Physical Standards for NOAA Special Agents and Enforcement Officers" dated 2023-10-25 exists as a PDF; content not read (read the landing page only).
- CBP: Criminal Investigator (GL-9 listed with age before 40, excerpt) appears in CBP eligibility sheet; CBP's published fitness test is for BPA/CBPO/AMO, not for criminal investigators; no 1811 test found (excerpt).
- Count of 1811 employers: GAO-07-121 (2006 data) says 71 LEO components used series 1811 (secondary, old). Current agency list should be taken from BJS Census of Federal Law Enforcement Officers; I did not find the series-by-agency table. Approximate list in this file = agencies I could evidence above, plus the unlisted ones.

## Body composition / medical fitness rules published for 1811 applicants
- OPM 1811 standard (from existing note, read there): moderate to strenuous exertion, firearms use, manual dexterity and joint mobility, hearing loss not over 35 dB (1000/2000/3000 Hz), near vision adequate, emotional stability, no condition that is a hazard to self or others. https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/1800/criminal-investigation-series-1811 (this session: only excerpt re-confirmed via EPA/IRS text).
- USSS: vision 20/100 uncorrected, 20/20 corrected; hearing 25 dB at 500/1000/2000 Hz (existing note; not re-verified).
- IRS: IRM 9.11.5 (effective 2026-11-20, replaces 2026-04-07) lists essential duties incl. wearing body armor up to 20 lb and requires meeting PPRs (read). Hepatitis B immunity checked at pre-placement exam.
- Body composition: no numeric entry cutoff found at any agency. Body fat percentage appears in the FLETC PEB, IRS-CI annual assessment, FWS annual assessment. 5 U.S.C. 3312 allows waiving height/weight for preference eligibles (existing note).
- Medical forms: ICE requires physician medical release and self-assessment form to take the PFT-P (excerpt).

## Implications for the tool
- Model each test as data: {agency, test name, events, scoring model, minimum values, banding (none/sex/age+sex), source URL, version/effective date, evidence level, last_verified}.
- Per-event minimum agencies (ICE, ATF, USMS, State DS, USPIS) can be checked as pass/fail directly if the user enters raw results and the standard is read-level. Point-system agencies (FBI, DEA, USSS) require an official points table: link out and only compute with user-entered points or read tables.
- Event definitions differ (sit-ups 1 min vs 2 min; sprint 220 yd vs 300 m; untimed push-ups at FBI/DEA vs 1 min elsewhere). Do not map one agency's reps to another.
- Show "standard changed on [date]" banners (FBI Nov 2025, DEA July 2026).
- Disclaimer: self-test only, not official, rules change; non-affiliation.

## Risks
- Stale or wrong pass marks mislead candidates (several agencies changed tests in the last 14 months).
- Excerpt-level evidence: ~70% of figures here come from search summaries, not page text.
- Different test definitions (reps/time) are easy to misapply.
- Publishing scoring tables may reproduce agency material (low risk, but cite and link).
- Safety: users self-testing push-ups/runs; add safety disclaimer.
