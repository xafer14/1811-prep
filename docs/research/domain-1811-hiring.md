# Domain research: GS-1811 federal criminal investigator hiring

Access date for every URL below: 2026-11-24. Method caveat: most agency career sites (fbijobs.gov, dea.gov, atf.gov, usmarshals.gov, secretservice.gov pages other than "qualifications") returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch. Facts attributed to those sites come from search-result excerpts of the official pages and are tagged (excerpt). Directly read pages are tagged (read). Re-verify every "(excerpt)" figure on the live page before publishing.

> Fitness-test events, minimums and PEB details are owned by [fitness-standards-federal.md](fitness-standards-federal.md) since 2026-11-24. Fitness statements in this note are marked "(superseded 2026-11-24)".

## Summary
- 1811 = OPM "Criminal Investigation Series"; the only OPM page I could read gives the medical standard, not the series definition or grade-level education/experience rules (those sit in OPM's group standard for administrative positions, not read).
- There is NO single 1811 entry age. Statute (5 U.S.C. 3307(d),(e)) lets each agency head set min/max entry ages; observed caps range 36 (FBI, DEA, State DS) to 37 (IRS-CI, USPIS, USMS, NCIS) to 40 (ATF, USSS). ICE/HSI removed its cap in Aug 2025 (secondary only).
- Preference-eligible veterans: 5 U.S.C. 3312 requires waiving age limits unless essential to the job; OPM 2009 guidance applied this to LEO entry age. Prior covered federal LEO service (5 U.S.C. 8336(c)/8412(d)) is the other common exception, usually by subtracting prior service. Wording differs per agency (e.g., USSS says veterans must still meet the age limit).
- Entry grades vary: USSS GL-07/09/GS-11; DEA GS-7/9/11; ATF GL-5 to GL-9; FBI GL-9/GL-10 (excerpt). LEAP adds 25% of basic pay for qualifying 1811s.
- Tests differ by agency: FBI Phase I computerized test + panel interview; DEA PTA then proctored writing assessment then interview; ATF Special Agent Applicant Assessment then PTT then interview; USSS written exam + Applicant Physical Abilities Test; USPIS assessment center + Applicant Physical Assessment. Fitness events and pass marks all differ.
- Polygraph and Top Secret background are near-universal but timing differs (ATF/USSS after tentative offer; DEA full-scope polygraph).
- Academy: FLETC CITP (59 training days, Glynco) is the common core; agencies add their own basic training (ATF ~15 wk, HSI 13-15 wk, NCIS 12 wk), or train separately (FBI 18 wk Quantico, DEA 16 wk Quantico, USPIS Potomac).
- Hiring is mixed: standard USAJOBS announcements, Schedule B excepted-service open-continuous listings, and direct-hire announcements where veterans' preference/rule of three may not apply.
- Many published figures conflict across pages of the SAME agency (FBI PFT points, DEA training length, ATF 37 vs 40, HSI academy weeks). The product must date-stamp and link to sources, not assert one number.
- No official source found that quantifies which stage applicants most fail; avoid claiming it.

## Findings

### 1. The 1811 series
- OPM title "Criminal Investigation Series, 1811"; its page (read) lists medical requirements: moderate to strenuous exertion, firearm use, manual dexterity and joint mobility, hearing loss not over 35 dB at 1000/2000/3000 Hz, near vision adequate for normal print, emotional stability, and no condition making the applicant a hazard. Source: https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/classification-qualifications/general-schedule-qualification-standards/1800/criminal-investigation-series-1811 (read).
- Same page says to use the Group Coverage Qualification Standard for Administrative and Management Positions with it (read). Series definition text and grade tables: not retrieved (unverified).
- Typical entry grades seen in current announcements: DEA GS-7/9/11, promotion potential 13, base $48,854-$82,938 plus locality plus 25% LEAP (read, https://www.usajobs.gov/job/861282700). ATF GL-5 to GL-9, $51,632-$84,579 rest-of-US base, promotion potential GS-13 (read, https://www.usajobs.gov/job/888615200). USSS GL-07 $49,508, GL-09 $55,214, GS-11 $64,009 starting (read, https://www.secretservice.gov/careers/special-agent/qualifications; page undated, figures may be stale).
- Pay: LEAP is 25% of basic pay for 1811/1812 criminal investigators averaging 2+ hours unscheduled duty per regular day, subject to a biweekly earnings cap in 5 U.S.C. 5547(c) (excerpt, https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/pay-administration/fact-sheets/availability-pay/). OPM's RFI (excerpt) discusses special rates for 1811 positions for January 2027; it is an RFI, not final (https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/latest-memos/request-for-information-special-rates-to-achieve-january-2027-pay-adjustments-for-certain-law-enforcement-personnel.pdf). Treat salaries as (unverified) for 2027.
- Typical education/experience paths (read, DEA and USSS pages): GS-7 = 1 yr specialized experience or 1 yr graduate study or bachelor's with Superior Academic Achievement; GS-9 = master's/JD, 2 yrs graduate study, or 1 yr GS-7-level experience; GS-11 = PhD, 3 yrs graduate, or 1 yr GS-9-level experience. Agencies layer their own rules (FBI requires a bachelor's, excerpt).

### 2. Maximum entry age
- Verified on 2026-11-26 from official pages the owner supplied: **FBI**: at least 23 years old at appointment and younger than 37, unless the person qualifies for an age waiver available to veterans (FBI FAQ page "How old do you have to be to become an agent?", printed 2026-11-26). **ATF**: at least 21 at application and not older than 40 at appointment; the day before the 40th birthday is the last day of selection consideration; the limit may not apply to preference-eligible veterans or to people who served in a federal law enforcement position covered by 5 U.S.C. 8336(c) or 8412(d) (prior service is subtracted from the age, and the result must be under 40) (ATF Special Agent Informational Packet, last updated 4 Aug 2026).
- Legal basis: 5 U.S.C. 3307(d) and (e) let an agency head set min/max ages for original appointment to law enforcement officer positions; (a) otherwise bars age limits (read, https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/3307). Note: 5 CFR 842.206 is NOT the entry-age rule (it covers involuntary retirement; read). The 5 CFR cite for LEO retirement coverage is Part 842 generally; I did not find the exact CFR section for mandatory separation (unverified).
- Veterans: 5 U.S.C. 3312 requires waiver of age/height/weight for preference eligibles unless essential to the job (read, https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/3312). OPM's 2009 CHCO transmittal reportedly applies this to LEO entry age after a 2008 MSPB ruling (excerpt via search; PDF was unreadable by fetch: https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/transmittals/2009/change-maximum-entry-age-requirements-veterans%E2%80%99-preference-eligibles_508.pdf). Suitability, qualification and medical standards still apply.
- Prior federal LEO service: announcements waive or offset the cap for people with service creditable under the LEO provisions of CSRS/FERS (5 U.S.C. 8336(c)/8412(d)); ATF says prior primary LE service is subtracted from age, then applicant must be under 40 (read, https://www.usajobs.gov/job/888615200).
- Direct-hire announcements: some state veterans' preference "will not apply" yet still exempt preference eligibles from the age cap (excerpt of USAJOBS results; check each: https://www.usajobs.gov/job/884668000).

### 4. FLETC Criminal Investigator Training Program
- 59 training days at Glynco, GA; FLETA accredited; for full-time agents/officers sent by partner organizations that set their own recruiting standards; attendees must meet FLETC Physical Performance Requirements on arrival. Areas: behavioral science, counterterrorism, cyber, driver, enforcement operations, firearms, investigative operations, leadership, legal, physical techniques (read, https://www.fletc.gov/criminal-investigator-training-program). Applicants do NOT apply to FLETC; the hiring agency sends them.
- PEB (Physical Efficiency Battery): see fitness-standards-federal.md (superseded 2026-11-24).

### 5. How hiring happens
- Standard USAJOBS announcements, including open-continuous ones (ATF open to 2027-09-30, read). Schedule B excepted appointments under 5 CFR 213.3210 at DEA and ATF; ATF converts to competitive after 3 years (read).
- Direct hire: several 1811 postings invoke DHA, where rule of three, category rating and veterans' preference procedures are said not to apply (excerpt of USAJOBS results).
- Agency portals: FBI (apply.fbijobs.gov), USPIS (IRAS portal), State (Foreign Service specialist process). DEA posts live announcements on set dates (public notice said 2026-07-20 and 2026-10-19; read).
- Veterans: 5/10-point preference lists ahead of non-preference in standard announcements (read, ATF). 30%+ disabled veterans have noncompetitive options under 5 CFR 315.707 (excerpt). Age waiver is separate from preference scoring.
- Hiring freeze: DEA notice says the position is exempt from the civilian hiring freeze (read).

### 6. Where applicants fail or get confused (official sources only)
- Limited official evidence. FBI states two interview failures disqualify and retest is after 6 months (excerpt). DEA warns resumes over two pages are removed and self-ratings exceeding evidence can exclude (read). GAO notes polygraph/background steps lengthen time-to-hire for CBP (excerpt, https://www.gao.gov/products/GAO-24-107029); not 1811-wide. No official fail-rate data found for any 1811 stage.
- Confusion visible from the sources themselves: age rules (cap vs waiver), conflicting fitness minimums, "veterans' preference applies / does not apply" in DHA announcements, GL vs GS grading.

## Implications for the product
- Content that is legitimate and useful: per-agency requirement comparison with source links and "last verified" dates; age-eligibility explainer (cap, vet waiver, prior LEO offset) with a disclaimer; process-stage maps; USAJOBS resume tips (specialized experience wording, two-page limit for DEA); generic fitness training plans for the event types agencies publish; checklist of disqualifiers (Lautenberg, Selective Service, tattoos at USSS, vision/hearing).
- Interactive tool candidates: (a) Eligibility and readiness screener that outputs agency-specific flags (age by agency, degree path, vision) and links to the live announcement; (b) fitness tracker that lets the USER enter the current official standards by agency with a source-link and edit option.
- Must NOT: reproduce any real or recalled test question, or "study guide" content derived from proprietary assessments; imply agency endorsement or use agency logos/seals; promise outcomes; give legal advice on waivers; claim pass marks as official unless linked and dated; present unsourced "fail rates".
- Because standards change mid-year (DEA guide versions 5, 6, 7 in ~14 months), embed source URL + verified date next to every number and a monthly review cadence.
