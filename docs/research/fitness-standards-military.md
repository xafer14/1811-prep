# Military fitness and body-composition standards (research)

All URLs accessed 2026-11-24. Marker key: (excerpt) = official .mil page returned 403 to fetch, content taken from search-result excerpts; (secondary) = non-primary; (unverified) = not confirmed from a primary document. The landscape changed heavily between Sept 2025 and Sept 2026; almost every service has a new test or body-composition rule in the last 12 months.

## Summary

- DoD-wide policy (Hegseth memos, Sept-Dec 2025; DoDI 1308.03 reissue reported 2026-08-31 (secondary)) drove: two fitness tests per year (active), sex-neutral male-based scoring with 70% average for ~80+ combat-arms jobs, and waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) < 0.55 as the common body-composition screen. Source: https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2026/09/09/pentagon-codifies-fitness-standards-across-branches/ (secondary). I could not fetch the new DoDI itself (403).
- Army: the Army Fitness Test (AFT, 5 events) replaced the ACFT as test of record on 2025-06-01; combat-MOS sex-neutral standard (350 total, 60 per event) in force 2026-01-01 active / 2026-06-01 Guard and Reserve (https://www.army.mil/aft/). A separate pass/fail Combat Field Test starts diagnostic 2026-04 (https://www.army.mil/article/291880).
- Army body composition: Army Directive 2026-13 makes WHtR < 0.55 the ONLY standard and discontinues height/weight tables and the tape test (https://home.army.mil/carson/8117/8345/8209/ARMY_DIR_2026-13_ABCP.pdf, read in full). No AFT score exempts; AD 2025-17 (465-point exemption) is rescinded.
- (confirmed 2026-11-26 from MARADMIN 613/25 and 066/26, see the section "Marine Corps and Air Force orders") Marine Corps: WHtR <= 0.52 screen (all sexes) from 2026-01-01, then body-fat evaluation for those over; sex-neutral PFT minimum 210 points for combat-arms MOSs (https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/ (excerpt)).
- Navy: PRT unchanged (push-ups, plank, 1.5-mile run) but now twice a year in 2026; BCA now uses sex-neutral WHtR step with 0.55 cutoff (NAVADMIN 264/25 (excerpt); OPNAVINST 6110.1L).
- Air Force/Space Force: new 100-point fitness assessment, composite >= 75 plus component minimums, WHtR = 20 points with no minimum; official scoring resumed 2026-07-01 per af.mil pages (conflicting 2026-09-01 elsewhere). 2026 scoring charts could not be located.
- Coast Guard: no new test found; COMDTINST 1020.8I body-composition screening (waist-circumference based) still in force, WHtR change "under review" as of ALCOAST 079/26 and 272/26 (excerpt).
- (superseded 2026-11-24, see Verification update: the Army AFT table, Navy Guide-4 and Guide-5A, the older Marine order and the Air Force scoring charts are now read) Official scoring tables are mostly 403-blocked or non-machine-readable; only the Army AFT table and Army AD 2026-13 were read directly. Everything else is excerpt-grade.
- Enlistment max ages (active duty): Army 35, Navy 41, USMC 28, USAF 42, USSF 42, USCG 41, min 17 (https://usa.gov/join-military).

### Navy PRT (Guide-5A, DEC 2025) and body composition (Guide-4, JUN 2026)
PRT: push-ups (2 min), forearm plank, 1.5-mile run (or alternate cardio); passed with Probationary (the lowest passing category, 45 points) or higher in every modality. Values below are for altitudes under 5,000 ft (Table 4-1); the same guide has separate, slower tables for 5,000 ft and above (Table 4-2).

| 65+ | 4 / 0:58 / 20:35 | 1 / 0:50 / 20:52 |

Body composition assessment (BCA), two steps: Step 1 divides the waist (at the navel) by the height; the ratio is rounded down to four decimals. At 0.5499 or less the Sailor is within standards and Step 2 is not needed. At 0.5500 or more, Step 2 estimates body fat from the height-waist difference (rounded height minus rounded waist) and the weight using Tables 2 and 3 of the guide; the maximum is 26% for men and 36% for women. Over the limit means Not Within Standards, which fails the physical assessment for the cycle unless the High-Performance BCA Exemption applies (Guide-1). Height is rounded up to the nearest half inch and weight to the nearest pound.

### Marine Corps (MCO 6100.13A with Change 4 of 23 Mar 2022 and an administrative Change 5)
Older order, partly superseded: it sets the minimum passing PFT score at 150 points total (3rd class 150-199, 2nd class 200-234, 1st class 235-300) and has the hybrid pull-up/push-up, crunch or plank, and 3-mile run (or 5,000 m row) tables, plus the combat fitness test. It has no waist-to-height rule. The 2026 changes (MARADMIN 613/25 and 066/26: sex-neutral 210 points for combat arms, waist-to-height 0.52) were NOT in the supplied documents and remain excerpt-level.

### Air Force (Tab 2, PFRA scoring charts, effective 1 Mar 2026)
Scoring charts for nine components: waist-to-height ratio (20 points), push-ups, hand-release push-ups, sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunch, forearm plank, 2-mile run, 20-m HAMR, and the 2-km walk. The file has no composite pass mark (75) or component minimums; those are in the governing manual (DAFMAN 36-2905), which was not supplied. The charts are effective 1 March 2026; the official-testing start (2026-07-01 vs 2026-09-01) is still unconfirmed.

Coast Guard documents, the 2026 Marine MARADMINs, and the Air Force pass rules. (updated 2026-11-26: the Space Force manual arrived, see the next section)

## Verification update 2026-11-26 (Space Force manual, Marine Corps and Air Force sources)

Numbers entered in `src/content/` on 2026-11-26 are owned by the data files; this section is the evidence record.

### Space Force (SPFMAN 36-2905, 26 Sep 2025, incorporating Change 3 of 24 Apr 2026; official, read)
- Body composition (chapter 9): the waist-to-height ratio must be below 0.55 and is cut off (not rounded) after two decimals. A ratio of 0.55 or more is followed by a body fat measurement (lab assessment, or a 2-3 site tape measurement): at most 26.0% for men and 36.0% for women. The standard is the same for every age and sex.
- Fitness (chapter 3): the Human Performance Assessment has three components: muscular strength (1-minute push-ups or 2-minute hand-release push-ups), muscular endurance (1-minute sit-ups, 2-minute cross-leg reverse crunch or plank) and cardiorespiratory (2.0-mile run or 20-meter HAMR). A member needs a composite score of at least 75 of 100 and a minimum in each component (maximum points: 60 cardiorespiratory, 20 endurance, 20 strength). The scoring charts are in the myFitness calculator and are not in the manual, so the per-component minimums are not entered.
- The 2-kilometer walk (Table 3.1) is a pass or fail assessment for members medically prohibited from running; it is entered. Its times are identical to the walk table in the Air Force chart, which suggests the two services share the standards, but the Space Force charts themselves were not seen.

### Marine Corps (MCO 6100.13A with Changes 4 and 5; official, read)
- Minimum rows of Tables 2-2, 2-4 and 2-5 are entered: pull-ups (female minimum worth 60 points, male worth 40), push-ups (worth 40 points, maximum 70), plank (1:10, the same for everyone and every age) and the 3-mile run, by age group and sex. A Marine also needs at least 150 points in total.
- Cross-check: the owner pointed to two Military.com articles (updated 2026-09-23). Its pull-up, push-up and 3-mile run minimums equal the order, except one value: male pull-ups, age 46-50, order 4, Military.com 5. The order is used.
- (superseded 2026-11-26, resolved by MARADMIN 066/26, see the next section) Military.com's body composition article still describes the older height and weight chart; the earlier excerpt-level note says the Marine Corps moved to a waist-to-height screen of 0.52 on 2026-01-01. This conflict was unresolved, so no Marine body composition rule was entered and the 2026 changes were listed as pending.

### Air Force (Tab 2 scoring charts, effective 1 Mar 2026; official, read)
- The lowest scoring row of each component carries an asterisk (push-ups 1 minute, hand-release push-ups 2 minutes, sit-ups, cross-leg reverse crunch, plank, 2-mile run, 20-meter HAMR). The chart text has no legend. The asterisk rows fit the known minimums (push-ups 30 and 15 for people under 25; run 35 of 60 points) so they are entered as the component minimums, with a caveat in the tool. This was an assumption until AFMAN 36-2905 was seen (superseded 2026-11-26: AFMAN 36-2905 para 3.7.4 confirms that results below the minimum repetitions or time score zero).
- Forever Wingman (a veteran's blog dated 2026-08-01) describes the basic training test (1.5-mile run, 1-minute push-ups and sit-ups; 60, 20 and 20 points; composite of 75 and a minimum in each component) and says basic training did not adopt the new 2026 test. It gives no minimum numbers, so nothing was entered from it. Its structure matches the Space Force manual.

## Verification update 2026-11-26, later: Marine Corps and Air Force orders

Numbers entered in `src/content/` are owned by the data files; this section is the evidence record. The owner supplied MARADMIN 613/25, MARADMIN 066/26 and AFMAN 36-2905 (file hashes in [live-page-checklist](../verification/live-page-checklist.md)).

### Marine Corps (MARADMIN 613/25 of 18 Dec 2025 and 066/26 of 26 Feb 2026; official, read)
- PFT: from 1 January 2026, Marines in a combat arms PMOS (list in the message) are scored with the male, age-normed table and need at least 210 points in total (70% of 300). Other Marines keep the sex- and age-normed standards of MCO 6100.13A (at least 150 points). Entered as the combat arms option of the Marine PFT data.
- Body composition (066/26, effective 1 Jan 2026): the height and weight requirement is replaced by a waist-to-height ratio standard of below 0.52 for every sex. Waist (measured at the navel, twice, lower value) and height are rounded down to 1/2 inch. Exceeding the standard leads to a body fat evaluation (tape test or BIA; the body fat limits are in MCO 6110.3A, which we do not have). Marines scoring 285 or more on the PFT and CFT are allowed 26% (men) and 36% (women); 250 or more adds 1 percentage point. Weight is still recorded in 2026 but is not used.
- The order's own maximum-waist table (heights 53 to 84 inches) allows a ratio of exactly 0.52 at some heights (for example 32.5 in at 62.5 in), although the text says "below 0.52". The tool follows the table; all 61 rows read from the PDF match the model.
- MARADMIN 613/25 (earlier in December) said the waist-to-height change was still pending; 066/26 made it effective 1 January. Military.com's body composition article (updated 2026-09-23) still describes the old height and weight chart, so it is out of date on this point. The earlier excerpt-level "0.52" is now confirmed.

### Air Force (AFMAN 36-2905, 24 March 2026; official, read)
- The assessment needs a composite score of at least 75 of 100 and a minimum in each physical component (body composition has no minimum). Maximum points: 50 cardiorespiratory, 20 body composition (waist-to-height), 15 muscular strength, 15 core endurance. Repetitions or times below the required minimum score zero, and meeting only the minimums does not reach 75 (para 3.7.4). This confirms the asterisk rows of the scoring chart as the component minimums.
- Components: strength (1-minute push-ups or 2-minute hand-release push-ups), core endurance (1-minute sit-ups, 2-minute cross-leg reverse crunch or plank), cardiorespiratory (2.0-mile run, 20-meter HAMR, or the 2-kilometer walk for members medically cleared not to run; walk times in Table 3.1 equal the Space Force Table 3.1).
- Body composition: waist divided by height, cut off (not rounded) after two decimals; the waist is measured three times, the three closest values averaged and rounded down to 1/2 inch. A ratio above 0.55 is high risk; with an unsatisfactory assessment it leads to a body fat assessment (BIA scale or 2-3 site tape): below 26% (men) and below 36% (women). The standard is the same for every age and sex.
- The Space Force manual gives 60 points for cardiorespiratory (Air Force: 50), so the two services do not share the same scoring charts; the Space Force charts stay pending.

### Point scales entered 2026-11-26 (Army and Air Force)
The owner supplied the Army Fitness Test Score Tables (approved 15 May 2025, effective 1 June 2025) and the USAF scoring charts (Tab 2, effective 1 Mar 2026). Both were converted with `pdftotext -raw` and entered as point scales in `src/content/point-standards/` (the data files own the numbers).
- Army: 0 to 100 points for five events by ten age bands and two columns (male and combat, female); a point level that is skipped in a column has no row, so the next lower result earns the lower level. All 100 sixty-point rows equal the minimums read earlier. The total needs 300 points (350 for a combat MOS), 60 in each event. The alternate aerobic events are pass or fail and are not entered.
- Air Force: half-point rows; each component's lowest row (marked with an asterisk) is its minimum and anything below scores zero (AFMAN 36-2905, 3.7.4). All 126 asterisk rows (7 events by 9 age bands and two sexes) equal the minimums read earlier. The waist-to-height ratio earns 20 to 0 points (0.49 or less to 0.60) and is the same for every age and sex. One event per part counts; the tool takes the best result entered for each part. Composite of at least 75 needed.
- Some Air Force plank times in the chart are written without minutes (":55"); the data stores them as 0:55.

- Marine Corps PFT (MCO 6100.13A Tables 2-2, 2-4, 2-5, owner-supplied PDF): pull-ups (up to 100 points), push-ups (up to 70), plank (age and sex neutral, 100 to 40 points) and the 3-mile run, by eight age groups. The tables have blank cells outside each age group's range, so each row's values were assigned to the columns that are active at that repetition count or time (from the Max and Min rows); every column's first row matches its Min row and minimum points (40, or 60 for female pull-ups), and all minimums equal the ones read earlier. Not entered: the 5,000-meter row, altitude tables, the abdominal crunches table (replaced by the plank), the Combat Fitness Test. The test needs 150 points (210 for combat arms) and each event at its minimum.

## Per-branch findings

### Army
- Entry screens: OPAT (gender-neutral, used for MOS qualification pre-ship) and the Future Soldier Preparatory Course (FSPC) for applicants over the accession body-fat standard by more than 2% and up to 6% (https://www.army.mil/article/267217, excerpt). Oct 2025 report says FSPC now serves physical OR academic track, not both (secondary, Task & Purpose via search; conflicts with 2023 army.mil). Current numeric OPAT minimums: not retrieved (unverified).
- In-service test: AFT, five events: 3-rep max deadlift (MDL), hand-release push-up (HRP, 2 min), sprint-drag-carry (SDC), plank (PLK), two-mile run (2MR). Standing power throw removed. Test of record 2025-06-01 (https://www.army.mil/aft/, 2026-11-24).
- Scoring basis: combat specialties sex-neutral, age-normed; total 350, min 60 points per event. Combat-enabling: sex- and age-normed, total 300 (per-event 60 per article https://www.army.mil/article/284799 excerpt). Combat standard effective 2026-01-01 (RA) / 2026-06-01 (ARNG/USAR). Table "Approved 15 May 2025, Effective 1 June 2025": https://www.army.mil/e2/downloads/rv7/aft/AFT_Scoring_Scales_250601.pdf. Columns are "M|C" (male / combat-sex-neutral) and F, in 5-year brackets 17-21 to "Over 62".
- 60-point raw minimums parsed from that PDF with pdftotext (layout is irregular; treat as (unverified) until checked against the PDF visually). Format M|C / F:
  - 2MR 17-21: 19:57 / 22:55; 42-46: 22:04 / 23:15.
  - SDC 17-21: 2:28 / 3:15; 42-46: 2:45 / 3:42.
  - PLK 17-21: 1:30 / 1:30; 42-46: 1:10 / 1:10.
  - (resolved 2026-11-24: MDL and HRP 60-point rows are in the Verification update) MDL and HRP 60-point rows could not be extracted reliably (sparse table, labels detached): (unverified).
- Combat Field Test (CFT): pass/fail, 7 events (1-mile run, 30 push-ups, 100 m sprint, 16 sandbag lifts at 65 in, 50 m two-can carry, 50 m movement drill, 1-mile run), 30 minutes, age/sex neutral, 24 combat MOSs; diagnostic period ~365 days from 2026-04-17, record testing 2027-04-17 (https://www.army.mil/article/291880 excerpt; https://www.army.mil/aft/).
- Body composition: AD 2026-13 (signed copy read): WHtR < 0.55 sole standard; >= 0.55 flagged and enrolled; measured twice per calendar year; confirmation measure by a different team same day; height/weight tables and tape test discontinued; no separation until a 180-day HQDA assessment; to be folded into AR 600-9 within 2 years; 7-day gap required between AFT/CFT and WHtR. The directive does not state its signature date in the extracted text (likely 2026; unverified). AR 600-9 (16 Jul 2019) remains the base regulation.
- Accession body composition: not found for Army in current form (AR 601-210 / DoDI 6130.03 not retrieved).

### Navy
- Entry: Physical Screening Test (PST) for special programs; navy.com chart lists male 17-19: 46 push-ups, plank 2x30 s (sic), 1.5 mi 12:15 (https://www.navy.com/joining/requirements, excerpt). This appears to be a recruit/entry reference, not the PRT; exact scope (unverified). Navy accession height/weight: screening table, applicants over it are body-fat measured at MEPS (excerpt of DoDI 6130.03 summary; unverified numbers).
- In-service: PRT = push-ups (2 min), forearm plank, 1.5-mile run (alternate cardio exists). Passing = at least Probationary in each event; five performance categories. Two cycles in 2026: 1 Jan-30 Jun and 1 Jul-31 Dec (https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Messages/NAVADMIN/NAV2025/NAV25264.pdf (excerpt), dated 30 Dec 2025). Pre-2026 failures reset for advancement/reenlistment.
- Scoring tables are age- and sex-banded (Guide-5A, Dec 2025: https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Support/Culture%20Resilience/Physical/Guide-5A%20Physical%20Readiness%20Test.pdf, 403 to fetch). Probationary minimums per bracket: (unverified). Secondary sample: male 25-29 push-ups 34, female 13 (secondary, usamm.com via search; do not use).
- BCA: Step 1 WHtR (sex-neutral) cutoff 0.55 (one Navy guide wording: "at or below 0.5499" passes); body composition then computed with sex-normalized equations; exemption for BCA failure only with overall Outstanding-Low and Excellent-Medium or better in all three events (NAVADMIN 264/25 excerpt; OPNAVINST 6110.1L CH-1). Codified details were not fully public at USNI report time.

### Marine Corps
- Entry: Initial Strength Test (IST): male 3 pull-ups or 34 push-ups (2:00), plank, 1.5 mi 13:30; female 1 pull-up or 15 push-ups, 15:00 (https://www.marines.com/become/requirements.html, excerpt). Plank minimum on IST: conflicting (see Contradictions).
- In-service: PFT (pull-ups or push-ups, plank, 3-mile run), CFT (movement-to-contact, ammo lift, maneuver-under-fire), both age/sex-banded under MCO 6100.13A; PFT window 1 Jan-30 Jun. Since 2026-01-01 combat-arms PMOS Marines must score >= 210 points (70%) on the male age-normed table (MARADMIN 613/25; https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/ (excerpt)). Other event minimums: (unverified); older male 3-mile minimum 29:10 for 17-20 (unverified, pre-2026).
- Body composition: MARADMIN 066/26 (change 2 = 073/26 reaffirms): WHtR <= 0.52 (waist at navel / height), sex-neutral, semiannual; over-threshold Marines get body-fat evaluation; tape (circumference) body-fat remains until BIA machines fielded; body-fat caps sex/age-normed, extra 1% allowance for 250+ PFT and CFT but not above 26% M / 36% F (https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4414812/marine-corps-revises-body-composition-standards/ (excerpt)). Effective 2026-01-01 per one excerpt; exact effective date of 066/26 (Feb 2026 issue) is ambiguous.

### Air Force
- Entry: no numeric pass/fail screen at enlistment beyond height/weight; BMT arrival fitness numbers found only in a ~2016 PDF (outdated, ignore). Accession: BMI range (17.5 or 19 to 27.5; conflicting) then body-fat if over; max body fat 26% M / 36% F (https://www.airforce.com/how-to-join/join-the-air-force excerpt; DAFMAN 36-2032).
- In-service (new, 2026): 100-point composite = cardiorespiratory 50 (2-mile run or 20 m HAMR), body composition 20 (WHtR, no minimum), muscular strength 15 (1-min push-ups or 2-min hand-release push-ups), core 15 (1-min sit-ups, 2-min cross-leg reverse crunches, or forearm plank); composite >= 75 and each non-BC component minimum met; Satisfactory 75-89.9, Excellent >= 90 (DAFMAN 36-2905 dated 24 Mar 2026, from search excerpt; https://static.e-publishing.af.mil/production/1/af_a1/publication/dafman36-2905/dafman36-2905.pdf, 403). Tested every six months; only one of the two must be a 2-mile run (secondary Task & Purpose).
- Timeline: diagnostic 1 Mar-30 Jun 2026, official scored testing resumes 1 Jul 2026 (https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Air-Force-updates-fitness-test-requirements, fetched; af.mil/Fitness excerpt); one other source says 1 Sep 2026.
- Scoring tables: age/sex banded historically; 2026 charts not retrieved, only the 2021 chart set found. Per-component minimum values: (unverified).
- BC: WHtR > 0.55 (Air Force wording) and failing the composite triggers Tier 2 body-fat assessment (DAFMAN 36-2905 excerpt).

### Space Force

## DoD body-composition and accession policy

- DoDI 1308.03: 2022 issuance lets services use body-fat calculation, WHtR, abdominal circumference, height/weight screening, or a combination (excerpt via airandspaceforces.com summary; esd.whs.mil PDF 403). Army AD 2026-13 cites it as "10 May 2022, incorporating Change 1, effective 25 June 2025". Reissue reported 2026-08-31 (secondary only). Reported content: WHtR < 0.55 for all; exceeders get a service-specific body-fat assessment, failure leads to remedial program; services may be stricter (secondary).
- DoDI 6130.03 Vol 1 (Change 6, 3 Feb 2026 per search excerpt) governs enlistment medical/accession standards; numeric height/weight/body-fat accession tables could not be read: (unverified). Services publish their own (AR 600-9 Tables B-1/B-2 for Army; MCO 6110.3A for USMC; Navy table + MEPS body fat; DAF BMI + 26/36% body fat).
- BMI usage: Air Force uses BMI at accession; Army and Navy use weight-for-height tables rooted in BMI; in-service BMI is not a stated standard in any service (WHtR replaced it).

## Implications for the tool

Deterministic and primary-grade:
- Army WHtR < 0.55 (waist / height); AFT per-event point lookup from the official table (age, sex or combat flag). Table is a clean authoritative source but needs manual or OCR-safe extraction (pdftotext layout is unreliable).
- Marine WHtR <= 0.52; Air Force/Navy/DoD WHtR 0.55 (excerpt-grade).
- Age limits and Air Force body-fat accession caps (26/36).
Volatile or unpublished:
- (updated 2026-11-24: Navy Guide-4 and Guide-5A and the Air Force charts are read; still missing: Marine 2026 event minimums, Coast Guard PRP events and WHtR rule, Space Force charts, Air Force pass rules) Navy Guide-5A, Air Force 2026 charts, Marine 2026 event minimums, Coast Guard PRP events and WHtR rule, Space Force charts: not machine-readable or not located.
- Many rules are directives that expire when regulations are revised (AD 2026-13 expires on AR 600-9 revision; Army CFT diagnostic until 2027-04).
- Combat-MOS lists differ (21 vs 24 MOS on Army pages; "80+" across DoD).
- Tape/body-fat fallback tables: Army discontinued; Navy, USMC, AF still use body-fat step with tables not retrieved.

## Decisions needed

1. Scope of MVP body-composition check. Options: (a) WHtR only with per-branch threshold; (b) WHtR plus legacy tape/body-fat step; (c) legacy height/weight tables. Recommendation: (a), with a visible "if over, your service does a further assessment" note for non-Army branches.
2. Which fitness tests to score. Options: (a) Army AFT only; (b) all branches; (c) tool shows official minimums only and links to tables. Recommendation: (a) for launch plus Marine/Navy where tables are verified; others as link-outs until primary tables are in hand.
3. Combat-MOS vs general standard. Options: ask the user; default general; show both. Recommendation: show both for Army and Marine Corps.
4. Data handling for scoring tables. Options: hand-transcribe with checksum review; OCR; link-out. Recommendation: hand-transcribe with a two-person check and store source version and effective date per row.
5. Accession vs in-service. Options: only in-service; accession screen too. Recommendation: include accession height/weight only after DoDI 6130.03 and service tables are read (currently unverified).

## Risks

- High policy churn; numbers may change before any release (Army AD 2026-13 says implementation guidance and separation review pending).
- Reliance on excerpts for Navy, USMC, AF, SF, CG.
- User harm: telling a candidate they pass when a table value is wrong. Mitigate with effective dates and "verify with recruiter" text.
- Army pages disagree on counts and some may predate April 2026 updates.
- Legal/neutrality: avoid implying official endorsement.
