# Military fitness and body-composition standards (research)

All URLs accessed 2026-11-24. Marker key: (excerpt) = official .mil page returned 403 to fetch, content taken from search-result excerpts; (secondary) = non-primary; (unverified) = not confirmed from a primary document. The landscape changed heavily between Sept 2025 and Sept 2026; almost every service has a new test or body-composition rule in the last 12 months.

## Summary

- DoD-wide policy (Hegseth memos, Sept-Dec 2025; DoDI 1308.03 reissue reported 2026-08-31 (secondary)) drove: two fitness tests per year (active), sex-neutral male-based scoring with 70% average for ~80+ combat-arms jobs, and waist-to-height ratio (WHtR) < 0.55 as the common body-composition screen. Source: https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2026/09/09/pentagon-codifies-fitness-standards-across-branches/ (secondary). I could not fetch the new DoDI itself (403).
- Army: the Army Fitness Test (AFT, 5 events) replaced the ACFT as test of record on 2025-06-01; combat-MOS sex-neutral standard (350 total, 60 per event) in force 2026-01-01 active / 2026-06-01 Guard and Reserve (https://www.army.mil/aft/). A separate pass/fail Combat Field Test starts diagnostic 2026-04 (https://www.army.mil/article/291880).
- Army body composition: Army Directive 2026-13 makes WHtR < 0.55 the ONLY standard and discontinues height/weight tables and the tape test (https://home.army.mil/carson/8117/8345/8209/ARMY_DIR_2026-13_ABCP.pdf, read in full). No AFT score exempts; AD 2025-17 (465-point exemption) is rescinded.
- Marine Corps: WHtR <= 0.52 screen (all sexes) from 2026-01-01, then body-fat evaluation for those over; sex-neutral PFT minimum 210 points for combat-arms MOSs (https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/ (excerpt)).
- Navy: PRT unchanged (push-ups, plank, 1.5-mile run) but now twice a year in 2026; BCA now uses sex-neutral WHtR step with 0.55 cutoff (NAVADMIN 264/25 (excerpt); OPNAVINST 6110.1L).
- Air Force/Space Force: new 100-point fitness assessment, composite >= 75 plus component minimums, WHtR = 20 points with no minimum; official scoring resumed 2026-07-01 per af.mil pages (conflicting 2026-09-01 elsewhere). 2026 scoring charts could not be located.
- Coast Guard: no new test found; COMDTINST 1020.8I body-composition screening (waist-circumference based) still in force, WHtR change "under review" as of ALCOAST 079/26 and 272/26 (excerpt).
- Official scoring tables are mostly 403-blocked or non-machine-readable; only the Army AFT table and Army AD 2026-13 were read directly. Everything else is excerpt-grade.
- Enlistment max ages (active duty): Army 35, Navy 41, USMC 28, USAF 42, USSF 42, USCG 41, min 17 (https://usa.gov/join-military).

## Per-branch findings

### Army
- Entry screens: OPAT (gender-neutral, used for MOS qualification pre-ship) and the Future Soldier Preparatory Course (FSPC) for applicants over the accession body-fat standard by more than 2% and up to 6% (https://www.army.mil/article/267217, excerpt). Oct 2025 report says FSPC now serves physical OR academic track, not both (secondary, Task & Purpose via search; conflicts with 2023 army.mil). Current numeric OPAT minimums: not retrieved (unverified).
- In-service test: AFT, five events: 3-rep max deadlift (MDL), hand-release push-up (HRP, 2 min), sprint-drag-carry (SDC), plank (PLK), two-mile run (2MR). Standing power throw removed. Test of record 2025-06-01 (https://www.army.mil/aft/, 2026-11-24).
- Scoring basis: combat specialties sex-neutral, age-normed; total 350, min 60 points per event. Combat-enabling: sex- and age-normed, total 300 (per-event 60 per article https://www.army.mil/article/284799 excerpt). Combat standard effective 2026-01-01 (RA) / 2026-06-01 (ARNG/USAR). Table "Approved 15 May 2025, Effective 1 June 2025": https://www.army.mil/e2/downloads/rv7/aft/AFT_Scoring_Scales_250601.pdf. Columns are "M|C" (male / combat-sex-neutral) and F, in 5-year brackets 17-21 to "Over 62".
- 60-point raw minimums parsed from that PDF with pdftotext (layout is irregular; treat as (unverified) until checked against the PDF visually). Format M|C / F:
  - 2MR 17-21: 19:57 / 22:55; 42-46: 22:04 / 23:15.
  - SDC 17-21: 2:28 / 3:15; 42-46: 2:45 / 3:42.
  - PLK 17-21: 1:30 / 1:30; 42-46: 1:10 / 1:10.
  - MDL and HRP 60-point rows could not be extracted reliably (sparse table, labels detached): (unverified).
- Combat Field Test (CFT): pass/fail, 7 events (1-mile run, 30 push-ups, 100 m sprint, 16 sandbag lifts at 65 in, 50 m two-can carry, 50 m movement drill, 1-mile run), 30 minutes, age/sex neutral, 24 combat MOSs; diagnostic period ~365 days from 2026-04-17, record testing 2027-04-17 (https://www.army.mil/article/291880 excerpt; https://www.army.mil/aft/).
- Body composition: AD 2026-13 (signed copy read): WHtR < 0.55 sole standard; >= 0.55 flagged and enrolled; measured twice per calendar year; confirmation measure by a different team same day; height/weight tables and tape test discontinued; no separation until a 180-day HQDA assessment; to be folded into AR 600-9 within 2 years; 7-day gap required between AFT/CFT and WHtR. The directive does not state its signature date in the extracted text (likely 2026; unverified). AR 600-9 (16 Jul 2019) remains the base regulation.
- Accession body composition: not found for Army in current form (AR 601-210 / DoDI 6130.03 not retrieved).

### Navy
- Entry: Physical Screening Test (PST) for special programs; navy.com chart lists male 17-19: 46 push-ups, plank 2x30 s (sic), 1.5 mi 12:15 (https://www.navy.com/joining/requirements, excerpt). This appears to be a recruit/entry reference, not the PRT; exact scope (unverified). Navy accession height/weight: screening table, applicants over it are body-fat measured at MEPS (excerpt of DoDI 6130.03 summary; unverified numbers).
- In-service: PRT = push-ups (2 min), forearm plank, 1.5-mile run (alternate cardio exists). Passing = at least Probationary in each event; five performance categories. Two cycles in 2026: 1 Jan-30 Jun and 1 Jul-31 Dec (https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Messages/NAVADMIN/NAV2025/NAV25264.pdf (excerpt), dated 30 Dec 2025). Pre-2026 failures reset for advancement/reenlistment.
- Scoring tables are age- and sex-banded (Guide-5A, Dec 2025: https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Support/Culture%20Resilience/Physical/Guide-5A%20Physical%20Readiness%20Test.pdf, 403 to fetch). Probationary minimums per bracket: (unverified). Secondary sample: male 25-29 push-ups 34, female 13 (secondary, usamm.com via search; do not use).
- BCA: Step 1 WHtR (sex-neutral) cutoff 0.55 (one Navy guide wording: "at or below 0.5499" passes); body composition then computed with sex-normalized equations; exemption for BCA failure only with overall Outstanding-Low and Excellent-Medium or better in all three events (NAVADMIN 264/25 excerpt; OPNAVINST 6110.1L CH-1). Codified details were not fully public at USNI report time.

### Marine Corps
- Entry: Initial Strength Test (IST): male 3 pull-ups or 34 push-ups (2:00), plank, 1.5 mi 13:30; female 1 pull-up or 15 push-ups, 15:00 (https://www.marines.com/become/requirements.html, excerpt). Plank minimum on IST: conflicting (see Contradictions).
- In-service: PFT (pull-ups or push-ups, plank, 3-mile run), CFT (movement-to-contact, ammo lift, maneuver-under-fire), both age/sex-banded under MCO 6100.13A; PFT window 1 Jan-30 Jun. Since 2026-01-01 combat-arms PMOS Marines must score >= 210 points (70%) on the male age-normed table (MARADMIN 613/25; https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4363803/marine-corps-announces-updated-physical-fitness-standards/ (excerpt)). Non-combat-arms unchanged. Plank 60-pt anchors seen: min 1:10, max 3:45 (older fitness.marines.mil page; (unverified) for 2026). Other event minimums: (unverified); older male 3-mile minimum 29:10 for 17-20 (unverified, pre-2026).
- Body composition: MARADMIN 066/26 (change 2 = 073/26 reaffirms): WHtR <= 0.52 (waist at navel / height), sex-neutral, semiannual; over-threshold Marines get body-fat evaluation; tape (circumference) body-fat remains until BIA machines fielded; body-fat caps sex/age-normed, extra 1% allowance for 250+ PFT and CFT but not above 26% M / 36% F (https://www.marines.mil/News/Press-Releases/Press-Release-Display/Article/4414812/marine-corps-revises-body-composition-standards/ (excerpt)). Effective 2026-01-01 per one excerpt; exact effective date of 066/26 (Feb 2026 issue) is ambiguous.

### Air Force
- Entry: no numeric pass/fail screen at enlistment beyond height/weight; BMT arrival fitness numbers found only in a ~2016 PDF (outdated, ignore). Accession: BMI range (17.5 or 19 to 27.5; conflicting) then body-fat if over; max body fat 26% M / 36% F (https://www.airforce.com/how-to-join/join-the-air-force excerpt; DAFMAN 36-2032).
- In-service (new, 2026): 100-point composite = cardiorespiratory 50 (2-mile run or 20 m HAMR), body composition 20 (WHtR, no minimum), muscular strength 15 (1-min push-ups or 2-min hand-release push-ups), core 15 (1-min sit-ups, 2-min cross-leg reverse crunches, or forearm plank); composite >= 75 and each non-BC component minimum met; Satisfactory 75-89.9, Excellent >= 90 (DAFMAN 36-2905 dated 24 Mar 2026, from search excerpt; https://static.e-publishing.af.mil/production/1/af_a1/publication/dafman36-2905/dafman36-2905.pdf, 403). Tested every six months; only one of the two must be a 2-mile run (secondary Task & Purpose).
- Timeline: diagnostic 1 Mar-30 Jun 2026, official scored testing resumes 1 Jul 2026 (https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Air-Force-updates-fitness-test-requirements, fetched; af.mil/Fitness excerpt); one other source says 1 Sep 2026.
- Scoring tables: age/sex banded historically; 2026 charts not retrieved, only the 2021 chart set found. Per-component minimum values: (unverified).
- BC: WHtR > 0.55 (Air Force wording) and failing the composite triggers Tier 2 body-fat assessment (DAFMAN 36-2905 excerpt).

### Space Force
- SPFMAN 36-2905 mirrors the DAF test; Space Force release says WHtR is used and body composition is not a scored component (secondary, Task & Purpose; https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4371955/ , 403). Threshold wording is ">= 0.55" in Space Force manual vs "> .55" in DAF manual (excerpt). Entry screens same as Air Force (DAF recruiting). Space Force jobs are not listed as combat arms (secondary).

### Coast Guard
- Entry: initial fitness and swim assessment in week 1 at Cape May; older recruit-guide graduation minimums: 1.5-mile 12:51 M / 15:26 F, sit-ups 38 M / 32 F in 1 min (https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/TCCM/Documents/Helmsmannew.pdf, undated, likely outdated; (unverified)).
- In-service: Coast Guard Physical Readiness Program announced via ALCOAST 007/26 (Jan 2026); event list not retrieved (unverified). The PFT is no longer a primary route to meeting the body-composition standard (excerpt). Boat-crew PFT is a separate test.
- Body composition: COMDTINST 1020.8I; two screenings per year (April, October); ALCOAST 079/26 says the instruction is under review due to a DoD WHtR requirement; ALCOAST 272/26 (Sep/Oct 2026) says more WHtR information to come (excerpts: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/40fea89 , .../427ee4d). Waist limits M 39 in / F 35.5 in came only from a commercial aggregator: (unverified).

## DoD body-composition and accession policy

- DoDI 1308.03: 2022 issuance lets services use body-fat calculation, WHtR, abdominal circumference, height/weight screening, or a combination (excerpt via airandspaceforces.com summary; esd.whs.mil PDF 403). Army AD 2026-13 cites it as "10 May 2022, incorporating Change 1, effective 25 June 2025". Reissue reported 2026-08-31 (secondary only). Reported content: WHtR < 0.55 for all; exceeders get a service-specific body-fat assessment, failure leads to remedial program; services may be stricter (secondary).
- DoDI 6130.03 Vol 1 (Change 6, 3 Feb 2026 per search excerpt) governs enlistment medical/accession standards; numeric height/weight/body-fat accession tables could not be read: (unverified). Services publish their own (AR 600-9 Tables B-1/B-2 for Army; MCO 6110.3A for USMC; Navy table + MEPS body fat; DAF BMI + 26/36% body fat).
- BMI usage: Air Force uses BMI at accession; Army and Navy use weight-for-height tables rooted in BMI; in-service BMI is not a stated standard in any service (WHtR replaced it).

## Enlistment age limits (active duty, min 17)

Army 35, Navy 41, Marine Corps 28, Air Force 42, Space Force 42, Coast Guard 41. Source: https://usa.gov/join-military (accessed 2026-11-24). Recruiting pages differ (Air Force 41 and "under 40" seen elsewhere); see Contradictions. Commissioning limits not retrieved (out of scope for time; open question).

## Special operations selection (names only)

- Navy SEAL: Physical Screening Test (PST): swim, push-ups, sit-ups/curl-ups, pull-ups, 1.5-mile run (https://aws.navy.com/joining/requirements/physical-screening-test; fetch returned 503, content from secondary summaries).
- Army: Special Forces Assessment and Selection (SFAS) (3 weeks, land nav and team movements); Ranger Assessment and Selection Program (RASP 1 for E-5 and below, RASP 2 for senior ranks) (secondary: military.com).
- Marines: MARSOC Assessment and Selection (A&S) (secondary).
- Air Force Special Warfare: Physical Ability and Stamina Test (PAST); Special Tactics A&S (secondary; https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/2297597/special-warfare-career-fields-update-entry-requirements/ listed, not read).
- No numeric standards recorded (post-MVP).

## Implications for the tool

Deterministic and primary-grade:
- Army WHtR < 0.55 (waist / height); AFT per-event point lookup from the official table (age, sex or combat flag). Table is a clean authoritative source but needs manual or OCR-safe extraction (pdftotext layout is unreliable).
- Marine WHtR <= 0.52; Air Force/Navy/DoD WHtR 0.55 (excerpt-grade).
- Age limits and Air Force body-fat accession caps (26/36).
Volatile or unpublished:
- Navy Guide-5A, Air Force 2026 charts, Marine 2026 event minimums, Coast Guard PRP events and WHtR rule, Space Force charts: not machine-readable or not located.
- Many rules are directives that expire when regulations are revised (AD 2026-13 expires on AR 600-9 revision; Army CFT diagnostic until 2027-04).
- Combat-MOS lists differ (21 vs 24 MOS on Army pages; "80+" across DoD).
- Tape/body-fat fallback tables: Army discontinued; Navy, USMC, AF still use body-fat step with tables not retrieved.

## Decisions needed

1. Scope of MVP body-composition check. Options: (a) WHtR only with per-branch threshold; (b) WHtR plus legacy tape/body-fat step; (c) legacy height/weight tables. Recommendation: (a), with a visible "if over, your service does a further assessment" note for non-Army branches.
2. Which fitness tests to score. Options: (a) Army AFT only; (b) all branches; (c) tool shows official minimums only and links to tables. Recommendation: (a) for launch plus Marine/Navy where tables are verified; others as link-outs until primary tables are in hand.
3. Combat-MOS vs general standard. Options: ask the user; default general; show both. Recommendation: show both for Army and Marine Corps.
4. Data handling for scoring tables. Options: hand-transcribe with checksum review; OCR; link-out. Recommendation: hand-transcribe with a two-person check and store source version and effective date per row.
5. Accession vs in-service. Options: only in-service; accession screen too. Recommendation: include accession height/weight only after DoDI 6130.03 and service tables are read (currently unverified).

## Risks

- High policy churn; numbers may change before any release (Army AD 2026-13 says implementation guidance and separation review pending).
- Reliance on excerpts for Navy, USMC, AF, SF, CG.
- User harm: telling a candidate they pass when a table value is wrong. Mitigate with effective dates and "verify with recruiter" text.
- Army pages disagree on counts and some may predate April 2026 updates.
- Legal/neutrality: avoid implying official endorsement.

## Contradictions

- Air Force official-testing start: 2026-07-01 (https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Air-Force-updates-fitness-test-requirements; af.mil/Fitness excerpt) vs 2026-09-01 (SAF DTM and Military Times, secondary). Not resolved.
- Air Force WHtR boundary: "> .55" (DAF manual) vs ">= 0.55" (Space Force manual) vs "< 0.55" standard (Army, DoD). Equal in intent, different at exactly 0.55.
- Army WHtR: AD 2026-13 treats 0.55 as fail ("less than and not equal to"); Navy guide wording "at or below 0.5499" passes. Consistent.
- Marine WHtR 0.52 vs DoD 0.55 (service stricter, allowed).
- Marine Corps IST plank minimum: 40-second plank vs 1:03 on marines.com officer page (excerpt); unresolved.
- Marine IST: marines.com plank-based vs older marines.mil crunch-based (older).
- Air Force BMI lower bound: 17.5 vs 19 (airforce.com pages).
- Air Force max age: 42 (usa.gov) vs 41 and "under 40" (airforce.com / 2023 policy update, excerpts).
- Army combat MOS count: 21 (AFT FAQ) vs 24 (CFT FAQ) (https://www.army.mil/aft/).
- DoDI 1308.03 date: 10 Mar 2022 (esd.whs.mil excerpt) vs 10 May 2022 (AD 2026-13 reference) vs reissue 31 Aug 2026 (secondary).
- Army accession: 2023 army.mil says FSPC dual-track; Oct 2025 report says single-track (secondary).
- Army 2MR row jump: 61 pts = 17:07 but 60 pts = 19:57 for 17-21 M/C in the same PDF (possible parse error; check the PDF).
